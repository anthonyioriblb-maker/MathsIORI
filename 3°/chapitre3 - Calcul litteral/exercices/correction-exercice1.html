Exercice 1
a. 3(x + 4)
= 3 × x + 3 × 4
= 3x + 12
b. 5(y − 2)
= 5 × y − 5 × 2
= 5y − 10
c. 7(2x + 1)
= 7 × 2x + 7 × 1
= 14x + 7
d. −2(x + 6)
= −2 × x + (−2) × 6
= −2x − 12
e. −4(3 − a)
= −4 × 3 − (−4) × a
= −12 + 4a
f. x(x + 5)
= x × x + x × 5
= x² + 5x
